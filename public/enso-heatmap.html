Ribbon: each bar is one ENSO episode under the ONI rule — the 3-month mean at or beyond ±0.5 °C for five consecutive overlapping seasons. Bar colour is the episode's peak. Hover for details.
σ panel: the spread of anomalies for that calendar month across the whole record. The taller bars near the end of the calendar year are ENSO's seasonal phase-locking — events peak in boreal winter.
Data: NOAA Physical Sciences Laboratory · HadISST 1.1 · Niño 3.4 region (5°N–5°S, 170°–120°W).
Pre-1950 values are based on statistical SST reconstruction; uncertainty increases before ~1900.
Event counts apply the ONI rule (3-month mean at or above +0.5°C for five consecutive overlapping seasons) to this HadISST series. NOAA's operational ONI/RONI uses ERSST on a different base period, so counts may differ slightly.
Model forecast (NOAA CPC / IRI / ECMWF consensus) issued ; those values come from a different SST analysis and base period than the observed grid.
