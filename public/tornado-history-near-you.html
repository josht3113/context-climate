"Recurrence" is a crude average (years of record ÷ tornadoes counted) — not a true statistical return period, and it treats reporting as constant over time when it isn't: weak, brief tornadoes have been reported far more consistently since widespread Doppler radar coverage arrived in the 1990s, which is also why a "Since 1990" toggle is offered above. Distances are measured to the nearest point of each tornado's path, straight-line, not along any road or terrain.
