CO₂'s Accelerating Growth Rate

The concentration curve isn't just rising — the rate of rise is itself increasing. Annual growth averaged ~1.5 ppm/yr in the 1990s and over 2.6 ppm/yr in the last decade.

Radiative Forcing & the Greenhouse Gas Index

NOAA's AGGI converts every measured gas into its warming contribution (W/m²), then indexes the total to 1990 — the Kyoto Protocol baseline year.
