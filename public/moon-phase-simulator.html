How to use this simulator

The Orbit View shows Earth, the Sun's direction, and the Moon's orbit from above. Drag the Moon around its orbit to change the phase, or drag Earth to spin it and change the observer's local time.

  • Moon Phase panel shows exactly how the Moon looks from Earth right now, rendered from the real Sun–Earth–Moon angle.
  • Horizon Diagram shows the Sun and Moon's position in the sky for an observer facing south, based on the current local time.
  • Elongation angle is the angle between the Sun and Moon as seen from Earth — 0° at new moon, 180° at full moon.
  • Lunar landmark shows a fixed point on the Moon's surface that always faces Earth, demonstrating tidal locking.
  • Sync to Today calculates the real Moon phase and approximate local time for right now, using your device's clock.
