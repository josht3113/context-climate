Saros series

A Saros is 18 years, 11⅓ days — 223 synodic months ≈ 239 anomalistic months ≈ 242 draconic months. Sun, Moon and node realign almost exactly, so the eclipse repeats with nearly the same character, shifted about 120° west in longitude (the extra ⅓ day) and drifting slowly toward one pole.
