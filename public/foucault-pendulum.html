Notes

  • Turning rate = 15.04°/hr × sin(latitude). 15.04°/hr is one turn per sidereal day (23 h 56 min 4 s).
  • Swing tempo on screen is fixed so the swing stays visible at every playback speed. Real Foucault pendulums swing once every 10–20 s (the Panthéon pendulum, 67 m long: about 16 s).
  • Idealized pendulum: no air drag or energy loss and a perfectly straight swing. Real exhibits use a drive to keep swinging.
  • 24 pegs, 15° apart, placed 7.5° either side of the starting swing direction.
  • Recorded rate = degrees turned ÷ hours elapsed for that run.
