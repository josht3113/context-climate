Methodology & caveats

What "overcast %" means: the share of routine hourly surface observations in a given month where the sky was reported fully overcast (or obscured, e.g. by fog) — not the average cloud cover fraction. A month at 60% means the sky read overcast in three of every five hourly reports.

Automated sensors have limits. Since the early 1990s most U.S. airports report sky condition from an automated ceilometer — a single upward-pointing sensor with a maximum reporting height around 12,000 ft. It can miss high cirrus above that altitude and, because it only samples the sky directly overhead, may not always represent scattered or broken conditions across the whole sky the way a human observer would. Overcast frequency typically drops when a station's ASOS is commissioned; where that date is known it is marked on each chart, and “normal” is taken from the same side of it.
