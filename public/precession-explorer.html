Which Season Gets Closest Approach?
Earth's axis wobbles like a spinning top (~26,000 yr cycle), slowly shifting which season lands near perihelion. Today NH summer falls near aphelion — Earth is farthest from the Sun in July, moderating our seasons. In ~11,000 years NH summer falls near perihelion — amplified insolation, stronger glacial forcing. This ~23k periodicity is clearly visible in ice-core records.
