Three clues that give a front away

  1. Temperature. Look for an abrupt change over a short distance.
  2. Wind direction. Look for a sudden shift in the barbs from one side of the boundary to the other.
  3. Dewpoint. Look for a jump in moisture — higher dewpoints mean more water vapour in the air.

A real front shows all three at once. One clue alone is usually a coincidence.
