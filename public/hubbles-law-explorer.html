Cluster distances and velocities are the classic demonstration values used in early textbook Hubble diagrams (Virgo, Ursa Major, Corona Borealis, and Bootes clusters), converted from millions of light years to megaparsecs at 1 Mpc = 3.262 million light years. Hydrogen alpha rest wavelength (656.3 nm) matches the value used in the Spectral Fingerprint Lab and Doppler Shift Explorer. The modern accepted Hubble constant range (about 67 to 73 km/s/Mpc) reflects an active, unresolved discrepancy between measurement methods known as the Hubble tension.
