The H-R Diagram

Temperature and luminosity — and the size hiding between them.

Click any two stars to compare them — or start with a pairing below.

Try a pairing

Notice the bottom axis runs backward — temperature decreases left to right. That's not a typo: early 1900s astronomers sorted stars by spectral line strength before they understood why those lines varied, and the order stuck. Diagonal lines mark constant stellar radius; since luminosity depends on both temperature and surface area, two stars can share a temperature yet differ enormously in brightness if their sizes differ.
