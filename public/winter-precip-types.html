Methodology: Winter = Dec 1–Feb 28/29 (meteorological DJF). "Measurable" excludes trace amounts. Rain days are inferred (total precip days − snow days) since ACIS daily records don't separately flag precipitation type, so true rain-day counts on days that also had snow are undercounted. Storm grouping merges consecutive calendar days of measurable snow into one event. Trailing-average trend lines use a fixed N-winter window ending at each complete winter.
