About this data. Normals reflect NOAA’s 1991–2020 climate normals (a smoothed 30-year daily average) — dot color follows the same temperature scale used across ContextClimate’s heatmap tools, and the curve itself is lightly smoothed to remove the whole-degree rounding steps in NOAA’s published values. Records reflect the station’s entire threaded period of record via NOAA/ACIS ThreadEx, which stitches together nearby station history so long-running sites don’t lose decades to a single station move or renaming. Some cities are only served by a shorter-record airport station — check the period-of-record note above the chart before treating record values as definitive.
