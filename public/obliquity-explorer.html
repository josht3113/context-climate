Why Summer — Not Winter — Matters
Ice sheets grow when cool summers fail to melt the previous winter's snow. Winter cold is never the limiting factor — the poles are always frozen in winter. Lower tilt → weaker summer sun at high latitudes → snow survives summer → albedo feedback → glaciation.
