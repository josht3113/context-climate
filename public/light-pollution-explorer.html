Simplifications

  • Clear, moonless night; sky drawn for Long Island (40.8°N) at both sites so only the sky glow differs.
  • Faintest visible star from zenith sky brightness (Schaefer-based SQM conversion); typical observer.
  • Brightening toward the horizon and atmospheric dimming use generic values, not each site's real light domes.
  • Milky Way brightness scaled from the Gaia map to approximate surface-brightness values.
