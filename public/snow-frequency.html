ℹ Frequency computed from all available snow seasons on record. For each calendar day, the bar height shows the percentage of years in which that day's observation met or exceeded the threshold. Missing observations are excluded from both numerator and denominator. The trend curve applies a Gaussian kernel smoother (σ = 12 days) across all daily values, weighting nearby days by a bell-curve function. Unlike a simple moving average, this naturally tapers to zero at the edges of the season and avoids distorting peak timing and height.
