🔬 Inferring the Liquid Outer Core

S-waves cannot propagate through liquids. The global absence of S-waves beyond 103° from any earthquake proved the outer core is molten iron-nickel — confirmed entirely through seismic evidence.

🌐 Why the P-Wave Gap Exists

P-waves slow from ~12.5 to ~9 km/s entering the outer core. Snell's Law bends them sharply toward vertical, deflecting them away from the 105°–140° zone.

⏱ The P–S Time Gap

P-waves always arrive before S-waves at the same station. Watch the gold dots appear first, then cyan. Seismologists use this gap to calculate earthquake distance.
