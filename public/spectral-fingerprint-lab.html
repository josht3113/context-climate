Why this matters

Line positions digitized from the NYSED Earth & Space Sciences Reference Tables (2026 Rev. Edition), p. 3 — "Emission Spectra of Some Elements from Stars." Hydrogen and helium lines are cross-checked against the Balmer series and standard He I catalog values; carbon, nitrogen, oxygen, and silicon positions are estimated to the nearest ~1–2 nm from the printed chart.
